Vladimir Putin
President of Russia
Born: 7 Oct 1952
In office: 2000–present
Party: United Russia

Putin is a Russian politician and former KGB intelligence officer who has served as President of Russia since 2000. He is the longest-serving Russian leader since Joseph Stalin.

He has been sanctioned by numerous Western governments and indicted by the International Criminal Court in 2023.
